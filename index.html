    Swim (Revised)

    May 12, 2014

    You were a pool of water. I stepped my foot in to feel the temp and felt my entire body rush with a sensation I longed to get again. I took another step into the dark waters and fell over and into you. Instead of drying off, I sat paddling to stay afloat. You've nearly drown me in your current. Each time, I forgave you and I continued my swim. As my body soaked up all of you, I pruned and I could not release myself from you. I craved you. For even when you dried up, you were still with me. Even as other rains have passed over, they never stayed long because they knew I could soak up no other waters. Instead of drying off and trying a different pool who could span so much wider than you, I was content with you.
